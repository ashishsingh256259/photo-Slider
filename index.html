About

Student developer.
Real-world builder.

Learning fast. Shipping faster.

I'm Ashish Singh, a 2nd year CSE (AI/ML) student at NIET Greater Noida, and a self-taught full-stack developer focused on building practical software products.

I enjoy taking ideas from concept to working interfaces, backend systems, AI integrations, and deployed applications — picking up React, Node.js, Firebase, Tailwind, and AI APIs along the way to turn ideas into systems that real users can interact with.

I'm currently exploring AI integrations, backend engineering, system design, and intelligent product development — trying to understand not just how to build features, but how to build them well. Projects like FORGE, ThermoGuard AI, and RESQ-X have taught me more about product thinking than any course could.

If I'm not coding, I'm usually researching tools, reading about system architecture, or entering hackathons to test what I've built against real problems. Still very much learning — and loving every step of it.
